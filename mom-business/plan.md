# Startup Plan / Checklist

A practical checklist to take one idea from `ideas.md` and actually start it in
Mangalore. Tick items as they're done. Not everything applies to every business
— skip what doesn't.

## 1. Decide
- [ ] Pick the one idea to start with (from `ideas.md`)
- [ ] Define the product/service in one sentence
- [ ] Decide who the first 10 customers are

## 2. Money
- [ ] Estimate startup cost (₹): ingredients/materials, packaging, tools
- [ ] Estimate monthly running cost (₹)
- [ ] Set a price per unit and check the profit per sale
- [ ] Decide the starting budget and where it comes from

## 3. Make it legal (as needed for the business type)
- [ ] Check if **FSSAI** registration is needed (any food business)
- [ ] **Udyam / MSME** registration (free, helps with loans & schemes)
- [ ] **GST** registration (only if turnover crosses the threshold)
- [ ] Open a separate bank account / UPI for the business

## 4. Set up
- [ ] Source suppliers / raw materials (see `research.md`)
- [ ] Sort out packaging and labels
- [ ] Pick where to sell: home, local shops, WhatsApp, Instagram, Swiggy/Zomato
- [ ] Decide delivery: self, local courier, or pickup

## 5. Launch & sell
- [ ] Create a WhatsApp Business number
- [ ] Create an Instagram page + take good photos
- [ ] Tell friends, family, and neighbours first
- [ ] Get the first 5 sales
- [ ] Ask early customers for feedback

## 6. Keep track
- [ ] Note sales and costs each week (a simple notebook or sheet is fine)
- [ ] Review monthly: what sold, what didn't, what to change
